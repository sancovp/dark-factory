# Contrarian Analysis Recipe

Type: Recipe
Composes: irony_lens + anti_herd_lens → contrarian analysis pipeline

## The Problem

Standing alone, irony_lens detects false confidence. Standing alone, anti_herd_lens counters herd mentality. Chained together, they produce something neither can alone: a structured path from received wisdom to heretical insight that SURVIVES the irony test.

## Composition

| Step | Skill | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | irony_lens | Detect false confidence and hidden assumptions |
| 2 | anti_herd_lens | Counter herd mentality and find the contrary path |

## Protocol

### Step 1: Apply Irony Lens (Irony Protocol)

For the received wisdom or dominant take, apply the five inversions:

1. **Confidence inversion**: What assumptions are hidden in the confident claim?
2. **Scope inversion**: Where would this catastrophically fail?
3. **Value inversion**: Clarity for whom? What does this obscure?
4. **Agency inversion**: Who loses power when this is accepted?
5. **Purpose inversion**: Who BENEFITS from this being believed?

**Output of Step 1:** A list of 3-5 exposed assumptions or hidden contexts.

### Step 2: Apply Anti-Herd Lens (Contrarian Protocol)

Take the exposed assumptions and run the contrarian search:

1. **First Principles Check**: What would a first-principles thinker reject?
2. **Herd Detection**: What does the majority assume that is now exposed?
3. **Contrarian Anchor**: What would the OPPOSITE person argue?
4. **Divergence Protocol**: Invert one key assumption from the herd take.

**Output of Step 2:** A contrarian reframe that survives the irony test.

### Step 3: Synthesize

Combine the ironic exposure with the contrarian reframe into a defensible position:
- It must survive the irony test (no hidden assumptions)
- It must survive the herd test (not the obvious dissent)
- It must be actionable, not just contrarian for its own sake

## Quality Gate

The pipeline works if:
- Step 1 exposes at least 2 assumptions the original take didn't acknowledge
- Step 2's output differs structurally from both the original AND the obvious contrary
- The synthesis is defensible in 3 sentences or fewer

## Rarity: Uncommon → Rare (chained lenses compound analytical value)
