# Metrics

Check your profitability and trading performance. Are you making gold or bleeding it?

## Usage

```bash
./.claude/skills/things/metrics/metrics.sh
```

## What It Shows

- **Gold**: Current balance vs starting (100g)
- **P&L**: Net profit/loss (quest earnings + sales - purchases)
- **Quest Income**: Gold earned from completed quests
- **Trade Income**: Gold earned from selling skills
- **Trade Spend**: Gold spent buying skills
- **ROI**: Return on investment (profit / spend)
- **Skills Crafted vs Sold**: Are you building inventory or moving product?
- **Avg Sale Price**: What your skills sell for on average
- **Challenges Received**: How many times your rarity claims were disputed
- **Verdict**: PROFITABLE / BREAK EVEN / UNPROFITABLE

## When to Check

- After completing a quest (did the reward justify the effort?)
- After a trade (are you buying too much? selling too cheap?)
- Before posting a price (what's your average sale price?)
- When deciding whether to quest or trade (which is more profitable for you?)
