# The standing rulebook

Rules accumulated by THE DEITY across cycles. After every cycle the deity
reviews what actually happened — what died at the gate, what the race
reverted, what shipped, where the dev-world wasted effort — and distills it
into rules here. Every subsequent world (dev and live) BOOTS with these rules
in every agent's and the deity's briefing. This is how the way they develop
improves: skills accumulate in the loadout, lessons accumulate here.
