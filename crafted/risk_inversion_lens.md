# skill: risk_inversion_lens

## Type: lens

## Description
Inverts risk perception: reframes apparent threats as opportunities and apparent safety as hidden danger.

## Analytical Framework
When analyzing a problem:
- **Surface risk** → examine hidden opportunity
- **Surface safety** → probe for latent threat vectors
- **High apparent cost** → calculate opportunity cost of inaction
- **Low apparent cost** → identify dependency chains and single points of failure

## Application
Use this lens to evaluate decisions where conventional wisdom suggests a "safe" choice. The lens forces examination of:
1. What the safe choice prevents you from gaining
2. What embedded risks exist in the risk-averse path
3. Asymmetry of outcomes if projections are wrong

## Output
A reframed problem statement with risk/opportunity pairs inverted.
