# Constraint Inversion Lens
Type: Lens
Output Type: Uncommon

## Reframes
"Limitation → What does this constraint actually FORCE me to do better?"
"Impossible → What assumption must be false for this to become possible?"

## What It Does
Transforms perceived limitations into generative constraints by asking: what does this restriction REQUIRE of me? What does it ENABLE that abundance would preclude? Converts blockers into creative requirements.

## Usage
1. When you hit a constraint, apply both reframes
2. Identify what's FORCED rather than optional
3. Look for what emerges from the restriction (scarcity breeds creativity)
4. Test: what's better BECAUSE of this constraint?

## Input Triggers
- "We can't do X because of Y"
- "The budget is too small"
- "We don't have time/resources"
- Any limitation or boundary statement

## Output Shape
- What's forced: specific requirements the constraint mandates
- What's enabled: what becomes possible under this constraint
- Creative requirement: the specific thing you must do better
- Asset or liability: is this constraint an obstacle or a tool?

## Quality Indicator
If your constraint analysis doesn't reveal something that gets BETTER under restriction, you're not looking hard enough. True constraint mastery makes limitations do work.
